# Shakshuka

1. Fry the onion, garlic and pepper in the oil until soft. Add the cumin.
2. Pour in the passata and simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Make four wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook until the whites set.
4. Crumble over the feta, scatter the coriander.
